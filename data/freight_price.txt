Land Freight Rate: Los Angeles Port to New York
1. Weight tier 0kg - 500kg
Base shipping cost: 1100 USD per shipment
Fixed fuel surcharge: 120 USD
One-time customs clearance fee: 80 USD

2. Weight tier 500kg - 2000kg
Base shipping cost: 1850 USD per shipment
Fixed fuel surcharge: 120 USD
One-time customs clearance fee: 80 USD

3. Weight tier over 2000kg
Base shipping cost: 2600 USD per shipment
Fuel surcharge: 200 USD
Custom clearance fee: 120 USD

Policy Note: All fuel surcharges adjust on the first day of each month according to international crude oil prices.
All rates only apply to domestic US land trucking transport.
